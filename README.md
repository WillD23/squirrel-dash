# Ru's Squirrel Arcade

The home page (`index.html`) is a menu for picking a game.

## Squirrel Dash

A retro arcade side-scroller made for Ru. A squirrel runs through an autumn wood, hopping over tree stumps, rocks and hedgehogs and collecting acorns.

### How to play

- **Squirrels:** after the title screen, choose Classic, Ninja, Rugby or School. Tap one to start, or use the arrow keys and space. The Squirrel button under the game changes it too.
- **Jump:** space bar, up arrow, or tap the screen. Hold for a bigger jump.
- **Acorns:** +25 points each.
- **Boing!** Land on top of a hedgehog or swan to bounce off it for +50. Chain bounces without touching the ground for 100, then 200. Stumps, rocks, bollards, crates and walls are safe to land on and run along; only running into their sides ends the run.
- **? boxes:** headbutt one from underneath to release a golden acorn and become Super Squirrel for 7 seconds, knocking anything in the way flying for +25. You can also stand on the boxes.
- **Speed:** Gentle or Zoomy, using the button under the game.
- **Levels:** Free build (drag the squirrel and walnut anywhere, saved in the browser) plus nine challenges with limited pieces: Gap, Cliff, Boing, Sky nut, Zipline, Seesaw, Cannon, Hedge and Grand course.

## Squirrel Course Builder

Inspired by Mark Rober's squirrel obstacle courses. Drag pieces onto the screen to build a course, press GO, and the squirrel runs it to reach the walnut.

- **Pieces:** branch, ramps, crate, tree (climbable), spring, fan, grass, zipline posts (the squirrel whizzes to the next post along), seesaw (3 wide, tips when the squirrel passes the middle), cannon (fires the squirrel in an arc) and log tunnels (go in one, pop out of its partner; tunnels pair up from left to right). Brambles are prickly and end the run.
- **The squirrel** runs right on its own, hops up single steps, turns round at taller walls and falls if there is nothing underneath.
- **Levels:** Free build (drag the squirrel and walnut anywhere, saved in the browser) plus four challenges with limited pieces: Gap, Cliff, Boing and Sky nut.

## Squirrel Skydive

Squirrels can fall from almost any height and be fine, so this squirrel jumps out of a plane. Steer it down through 2,500 m of sky.

- **Controls:** left and right arrows to steer, up (or space) to glide slowly, down to dive fast. On a phone or tablet use the buttons under the game, or hold either side of the screen.
- **Dodge:** birds and storm clouds up high, hot air balloons in the middle, kites near the ground. Drones join in from jump 2.
- **Hearts:** 3 hearts, with a short flashing safe time after each bump. A golden acorn gives a heart back.
- **Acorns:** +10 points each.
- **Landing:** a bouncing arrow marks the leaf pile. A perfect landing in the pile is +500, a near miss +250, and every finished jump +200. Each jump falls a little faster than the last.

## Running it

- `index.html`: the game menu
- `dash.html`: Squirrel Dash
- `course.html`: Squirrel Course Builder
- `skydive.html`: Squirrel Skydive

Each game is a single file. Open any of them in a web browser, or play online through GitHub Pages.
