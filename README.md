# Squirrel Dash

A retro arcade side-scroller made for Ru. A squirrel runs through an autumn wood, hopping over tree stumps, rocks and hedgehogs and collecting acorns.

## How to play

- **Jump:** space bar, up arrow, or tap the screen. Hold for a bigger jump.
- **Acorns:** +25 points each.
- **Speed:** Gentle or Zoomy, using the button under the game.
- **Levels:** Autumn Wood, or Winchester (the cathedral, Great Hall, Westgate, King Alfred and the Buttercross, with bollards, market crates, flint walls and swans to jump). Use the Level button, or the left and right arrows on the title screen.

## Running it

Everything is in `index.html`. Open it in any web browser, or play it online through GitHub Pages.
