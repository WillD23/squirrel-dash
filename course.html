SQUIRREL COURSE
BUILDER

Build a course. Press GO. Get the walnut!

◀ ALL GAMES

Drag a piece from the box onto the screen, or tap a piece then tap the screen. Use Move to drag pieces around (drag one off the screen to put it back). The squirrel runs right, hops up single steps, climbs trees, bounces on springs and rides fans. It grabs a zipline post and whizzes to the next post, gets fired from cannons, and pops out of the matching log tunnel. Seesaws tip when it walks past the middle. Big walls make it turn round, and brambles are prickly!
